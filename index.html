01. About Me

I'm a Data Scientist & ML Engineer who loves building end-to-end AI systems — from cleaning messy datasets and designing feature pipelines, to shipping models behind production-grade APIs and dashboards.

My recent work spans real estate price prediction (CatBoost, Optuna, 0.867 R²), LLM evaluation platforms (RAGAS, LangGraph, FastAPI), and NLP sentiment analysis (RoBERTa fine-tuning). I care equally about the model quality and the infrastructure that runs it.

When I'm not training models, I'm adding Prometheus metrics, writing GitHub Actions pipelines, or reading papers on evaluation frameworks for AI agents.
